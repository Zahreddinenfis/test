Why Carthage Drive?

Germany needs professional bus and truck drivers. Tunisia has motivated, experienced drivers looking for a stable future. Carthage Drive connects both – honestly, transparently and with real support, not just a job placement.

Your journey, step by step

We stay by your side from the first conversation until you drive your first route in Germany.

  1. 01

    Selection

    Interview, driving experience check and motivation assessment. We only continue with candidates we truly believe in.

  2. 02

    Documents & visa

    We guide you through the paperwork: contracts, recognition of qualifications, visa application and medical checks.

  3. 03

    German language

    Language preparation in Tunisia so you arrive ready to communicate, study for your licence and settle in.

  4. 04

    Arrival & settling in

    Help with arrival, housing, registration, bank account and the first weeks in a new country.

  5. 05

    German licence

    Coordination of driving school, theory and practical exams and the professional driver qualification (Code 95).

  6. 06

    Start working

    You start your job with a German employer – and we remain your contact for questions along the way.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to speak German?

No, you start from your current level. We help you learn German before and after arrival.

Will I get a German licence?

Yes, that is part of the programme. We coordinate the driving school and exams so you can legally drive professionally in Germany.

Do I have to pay for the service?

Conditions are explained transparently in a personal conversation before you commit to anything. Please contact us for details.

How long does the process take?

It depends on visa processing, language level and exam dates. We give you a realistic timeline after your first interview.
